ABOUT THE TRUST

Serving people with dignity, kindness and respect.

BSRG Trust is a nonprofit organization based in Bharatpur, Nepal. Through elderly day care, religious activities and affordable visitor accommodation, the Trust aims to provide a welcoming place for people to connect, participate and find support.

⌖ Nagarban Marga, Bharatpur Metropolitan City–3, Chitwan, Nepal

A PLACE TO STAY

Affordable accommodation for pilgrims and visitors.

The Trust plans to make rooms in its building available at a reasonable price for pilgrims and other visitors. The current planning estimate is approximately 15 rooms; final availability, facilities and rates will be confirmed before bookings open.
