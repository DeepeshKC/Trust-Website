ABOUT BAA AAMA GHAR

A Place of Comfort, Connection, and Belonging

Located in Bharatpur, Chitwan, Baa Aama Ghar (बा आमा घर) is a welcoming space dedicated to comfort, companionship, and spiritual well-being.

More than a place to stay, Baa Aama Ghar brings together affordable visitor accommodation, elderly day care, and opportunities for spiritual reflection under one roof.

With a peaceful, homelike atmosphere, it offers older adults a place to connect, participate in meaningful daily activities, and feel valued and supported. Visitors and pilgrims can also enjoy a comfortable stay in an environment shaped by care, respect, and a sense of belonging.

Baa Aama Ghar is operated by Buddhi Man Smriti Ramhari Gangadevi Shrestha Paropakar Trust (BSRG Trust), a nonprofit organization committed to community well-being.

Guest Guidelines

To maintain the peaceful and spiritually welcoming atmosphere of Baa Aama Ghar, we kindly request that all guests respect the following guidelines:

  • Alcohol is strictly prohibited on the premises.

CARE & SPIRITUAL WELL-BEING

A Place to Connect, Reflect, and Belong

At Baa Aama Ghar, we believe that companionship, meaningful engagement, and spiritual well-being are essential to a fulfilling life.

Our elderly day care provides a peaceful, homelike environment where older adults can spend time together, build connections, and participate in everyday activities that support their mental and emotional well-being.

We also encourage spiritual awareness and reflection, creating opportunities for individuals to find peace, connect with their beliefs, and experience a sense of belonging.

Through care, companionship, and a welcoming atmosphere, Baa Aama Ghar strives to make every day meaningful.
